Mackyv3Version updatev3Aug 22, 2026

Why we rebuilt it

The original Macky was a chat tool: ask it a business question, get an answer back. Useful, but not enough. What customers kept asking for wasn't more advice — it was a finished piece of work. A real plan. A real audit. Something they could actually hand to their team or their board.

So that's what we rebuilt Macky to do.

What's new

Fixed-price consulting engagements. Choose from a growing catalogue — Pricing Review, AI-Adoption Roadmap, Go-to-Market Plan, CX Audit & Roadmap, and more — each scoped, priced upfront, and delivered on a set timeline. A CX Audit & Roadmap that runs $40,000+ at a traditional firm is $6,500 on Macky.
Better AI under the hood. We’ve moved from OpenAI to Anthropic’s Claude models, producing noticeably higher-quality, more accurate business writing and analysis — across every engagement and every chat.
A real consultant signs off on every deliverable. AI drafts it; a named, certified senior consultant reviews it before it ever reaches you.
Rework guarantee. Not right the first time — we fix it, at no extra charge.
Ask Macky, reimagined. Attach your own files — financials, decks, reports — for Macky to analyze directly. Ask for comprehensive, board-ready reports instead of a few paragraphs. Get them back as a properly formatted Word document, Excel model, or whichever format fits.
Your language, your choice. Request your outputs in the language you actually work in.
A workspace to run it all. Engagement tracking, a document library that holds everything you’ve ever generated, and team access if you’re not working solo.
Free Business Health Check. A five-part diagnostic of your business — free, any time you want a read on where things stand.
